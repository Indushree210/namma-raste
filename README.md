# Namma-Raste Health – Infrastructure Road Maintenance Tracker

## Overview
Namma-Raste Health is an Android application developed to improve rural road maintenance and infrastructure transparency. The app helps citizens report road damages such as cracks, potholes, and water logging before they become major issues.

The project creates a “Digital Life Book” for every road by storing contractor information, warranty details, and maintenance records.

---

# Problem Statement

Rural roads built under PMGSY and other government schemes often get damaged quickly due to:

- Water logging
- Heavy overloaded vehicles
- Lack of maintenance
- Delayed reporting of small damages
- Lack of contractor transparency

Citizens usually do not know:
- Who built the road
- Warranty details
- How to report damages

This leads to:
- Poor road quality
- Increased repair costs
- Unsafe transportation
- Faster infrastructure failure

---

# Solution

Namma-Raste Health provides:

- Road health monitoring dashboard
- Damage reporting system
- GPS-based complaint tracking
- Contractor information access
- Analytics for road conditions
- Community-based infrastructure monitoring

The app helps authorities identify damaged roads early and increases public participation in infrastructure maintenance.
